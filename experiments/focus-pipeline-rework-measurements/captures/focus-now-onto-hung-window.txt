Focus-now onto a hung window

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the pin) in session 1, eSpeak NG, default symbol and verbosity levels, through cargo xtask nvda capture. Mockapp ran from driver.py: `mockapp --fixture crates/mockapp/tests/fixtures/tree.json --backend uia --title "Rework UIA Hung" --show`. Driver times are microseconds since the Unix epoch.

What it shows. Mockapp was brought to the front with its focus on the OK button, the capture switched to Notepad, `stall 30000` blocked mockapp's window thread, and 5 seconds later Alt+Tab brought mockapp back while it was hung. NVDA said nothing: only a cancel at the key, nothing for the foreground window, plain or otherwise, and nothing when the stall ended 25 seconds later, within the capture's 40 second quiet period. Mockapp raises no focus event of its own when activated (none was armed, as a hung application raises none), so the only events were Windows' own foreground change and system focus on the hung window. Windows had mockapp in front at the end, as the final foreground check shows. NVDA's focus-now onto a hung window is therefore silence, not the window named by its text.
