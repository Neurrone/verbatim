A cancelled UIA parent read

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the nvda submodule's pin, 732d1ce2f0f051da3ef1e6a6247c64cf04596337) in session 1, eSpeak NG, symbol and verbosity levels at their defaults, delayed character descriptions off, through cargo xtask nvda capture. Mockapp ran from driver.py (this folder), which held its stdin: `mockapp --fixture crates/mockapp/tests/fixtures/tree.json --backend uia --title "Rework UIA Parent" --show`. Times in the driver lines are microseconds since the Unix epoch, the clock of mockapp's own stamps; capture times are milliseconds after the key or step was sent.

What it shows. A 1 ms `slow-parent` counting run showed that a focus on the list item `item1` makes six `Navigate(Parent)` calls on it: two inside mockapp's own `UiaRaiseAutomationEvent`, before its `applied`, and four from the client after it. "After its first parent step" is therefore `slow-parent item1 <ms> 3`: the raise's two calls and the client's first step pass, and every later call waits. With a 4 second wait, NVDA, arriving by Alt+Tab or within the application, says the window (Alt+Tab only) and "Items list" after two late calls, about 8 seconds, and never says the item, although the client's third late call ends within the capture's quiet period; this was the same in two runs from within. With a 15 second wait, NVDA says the same after two late calls, about 30 seconds, and "First 1 of 2" after the third, at 45 seconds by Alt+Tab and together with the list at 32 seconds from within. NVDA neither speaks the focus before its ancestors are read nor substitutes a window for the slow parent: it waits for every call, three in all, each paid in full, and says nothing until then. Within the application, Tab does not move mockapp's focus, so the focus was moved by `focus item1` from the driver, as the key would move it in a real application; by Alt+Tab, mockapp raised the focus on its activation (`on-activate focus item1`), since a mockapp window that raises no focus event is not announced at all when it comes to the front (the `--front` lines below). Two earlier runs by Alt+Tab with `after` 1, which stalled mockapp's own raise instead (the second raise call), are kept at the end: with the raise stalled 15 seconds, past the hung judgement, NVDA said "unknown" at 5 seconds, then the window and list at 60 seconds and the item at 75 seconds, after four more late calls of its own.
