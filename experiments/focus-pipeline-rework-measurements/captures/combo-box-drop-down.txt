A combo box's drop-down

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the pin) in session 1, eSpeak NG, default symbol and verbosity levels, through cargo xtask nvda capture with a 2 second quiet period. The window is forms-list.ps1 (this folder) with `-Kind combobox`: a Windows Forms ComboBox in DropDownList style named "Letters", holding Alpha, Beta and Gamma with Alpha selected, in a form titled "Rework ComboBox", in the form of the text_box_password scenario's script. dropdown-chain.ps1 (this folder) read the drop-down while it was open after Alt+Down: the process's visible ComboLBox window, its window relations, its MSAA accParent chain from its client object, and its UIA raw-view parent chain (System.Windows.Automation's RawViewWalker, the .NET Framework client over UI Automation's core).

What it shows. The drop-down list is a top-level ComboLBox window of the form's process with no owner, its parent the desktop window (#32769), and the keyboard focus stays in the combo box while it is open. Its MSAA accParent chain reaches the desktop through the combo box and the combo box's top-level window: list "Letters", its window, combo box "Letters", its window, the form's client and window "Rework ComboBox", then the desktop's client and window, named "Desktop 1". Its UIA raw-view parent is the desktop root directly, with no combo box or form between, and UIA gives the pane no name. So the MSAA chain, which NVDA follows for this window, meets the combo box's chain at the combo box, while the raw view does not confirm that parent. NVDA's speech for Alt+Down is "expanded" and nothing else, with no cancel after the key's own; Down Arrow in the open list then says "Letters list" and "Beta". Alt+Up said nothing after the key's cancel, and a second Alt+Down and Escape said nothing.
