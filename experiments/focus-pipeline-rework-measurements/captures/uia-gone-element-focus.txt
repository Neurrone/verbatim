A gone UIA element's focus

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the pin) in session 1, eSpeak NG, default symbol and verbosity levels, through cargo xtask nvda capture. Mockapp ran from driver.py: `mockapp --fixture crates/mockapp/tests/fixtures/tree.json --backend uia --title "Rework UIA Gone" --show`, brought to the front by the capture's `--front`, the focus first on `btn_ok` by `focus btn_ok`. Driver times are microseconds since the Unix epoch.

What it shows. When the editable text node `edit1` raises its focus and the window thread stalls at once for 2 seconds, killing the node 100 ms into the stall (`stall 2000 100 disconnect edit1`), NVDA says nothing for that focus: no "Notes edit", no other object in its place, and the previous focus is not said again; in one of the two runs it queued a cancel at the stall's end and nothing else. The same focus without the stall says "Notes edit Hello world". So NVDA's object for a gone UIA element is none: the event is dropped. The first run, below, began its commands about 18 ms after the capture command started, possibly before the add-on's client connected, so the second run, with a 1.5 second delay first, is the one to take; both agree.
