A repeated MSAA focus on a simple child

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the pin) in session 1, eSpeak NG, default symbol and verbosity levels, through cargo xtask nvda capture with a 2 second quiet period. The windows are forms-list.ps1 (this folder), in the form of the text_box_password scenario's Windows Forms script: `-Kind listview`, a ListView in details view, and `-Kind listbox`, a ListBox, each named "Letters" and holding Alpha, Beta and Gamma with the first item focused and selected, launched by the capture's `--launch powershell.exe ... -WindowStyle Hidden -File forms-list.ps1`. winevent-hook.ps1 (this folder), an out-of-context hook for EVENT_OBJECT_FOCUS (0x8005) on every process, recorded the focus events raised meanwhile.

What it shows. Home on the first item, already focused, raises no focus event in either control on this Windows build: the hook records none, and NVDA only cancels speech for the key and says nothing, both times it is pressed. Home after Down Arrow raises one focus event on child 1 and NVDA says the item. So Home on the first item does not give a repeated MSAA focus on a simple child here. The one repeat these windows raise comes when they open: the control's focus event on child 0 (the list itself) and then on child 1 (Alpha), in the same tick. For the ListView NVDA says "Alpha 1 of 3" twice, once for the list's focus, which it takes to the focused item, and once for child 1, not taking the second as a duplicate; for the ListBox it says "Alpha" once.
