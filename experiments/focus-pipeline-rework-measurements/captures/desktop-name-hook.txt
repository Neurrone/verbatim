The virtual desktop name's hook

Taken on 2026-10-30 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), in session 1, whose csrss.exe is process 980, with NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 running and Verbatim not. The machine had two desktops, both unnamed, before and after; none was added, so none had to be closed. keys.ps1 (this folder) pressed Windows+Control+Right and Left with SendInput. winevent-hook.ps1 (this folder) installed an out-of-context hook (WINEVENT_OUTOFCONTEXT) for EVENT_OBJECT_NAMECHANGE (0x800C), once scoped to csrss.exe (idProcess 980) and once, at the same time, to every process, both recording only the window GetDesktopWindow() (65548, class #32769) with its MSAA accName; a third run, scoped to csrss.exe with no window filter, recorded every name change csrss raised for 30 seconds. desktop-accname.ps1 (this folder) timed AccessibleObjectFromWindow on GetDesktopWindow() with OBJID_CLIENT and the accName read, first with a responsive Windows Forms window in front, then 24 times, 250 ms apart, from 1.9 to 8.0 seconds into mockapp's `stall 10000` with mockapp in front (mockapp --fixture crates/mockapp/tests/fixtures/tree.json --backend uia --title "Rework Desktop Stall" --show, held by driver.py). Times are microseconds since the Unix epoch; the hook and key logs take theirs from .NET Framework's DateTime.UtcNow, which is coarser than a microsecond.

What it shows. The hook scoped to csrss.exe installs (SetWinEventHook returned a handle) and delivered the desktop window's name change for every switch, 4 of 4 and then 2 of 2, each 30 to 52 ms after the key, with the same event stamps as the hook on every process; the accName read in the callback gave the desktop switched to ("Desktop 2", "Desktop 1"). In 30 seconds the csrss-scoped hook received no event but the two switches' name changes, so it adds no wake-ups beyond them. The raising thread's process shows as unknown because OpenThread is refused on csrss.exe's thread; the window's process is 980. The accName read does not wait on the stalled foreground application: during the stall, with mockapp judged hung from 5.2 seconds on and its ghost window in front, AccessibleObjectFromWindow took 65 to 1,139 us and accName 0.80 to 45.6 ms, median 1.2 ms, every read answering "Desktop 1"; with a responsive window in front they took 66 to 1,188 us and 1.0 to 3.4 ms. The read of about a millisecond suggests a call into another process, which this check did not identify, so a stall of whichever process serves the name, rather than of the foreground application, was not tested.
