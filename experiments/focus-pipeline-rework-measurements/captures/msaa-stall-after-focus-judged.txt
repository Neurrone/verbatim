A stall after an MSAA focus is judged

Taken on 2026-10-29 on Windows 11 build 26200 (x64), NVDA alpha-58022,732d1ce2 (the pin) in session 1, eSpeak NG, default symbol and verbosity levels, through cargo xtask nvda capture. Mockapp ran from driver.py: `mockapp --fixture crates/mockapp/tests/fixtures/tree.json --backend msaa --title "Rework MSAA Parent" --show`, brought to the front by `--front`. Driver times are microseconds since the Unix epoch.

What it shows. Mockapp does not move its focus on Tab, so the move to the list item was raised by `focus item1` from the driver, as Tab would raise it in a real application, after a focus on another control. NVDA's first call on the item's new object is `accParent`, so its first call reaches the provider twice in a row (the trap in docs/tooling/mockapp.md): in every run the late calls come in back-to-back pairs, the first pair being NVDA's first parent call and the second its next object's, so each NVDA parent call costs twice the `slow-acc-parent` time. Nothing reads the item's name in NVDA's proxy before its parent, and no other client can change that, so the doubled time is reported. With `slow-acc-parent item1 2000`, 4 seconds per NVDA call and so short of the hung judgement, NVDA waits for every call, four provider calls and 8 seconds in all, and then says "Items list" and "First", the same speech as with no wait. With `slow-acc-parent item1 4000`, 8 seconds per call, and with 15000, 30 seconds per call, both past the hung judgement, NVDA makes only its first parent call, never a second, and says nothing for the item at all, within quiet periods of 30 and 60 seconds; its focus stays on the previous control (a later `focus btn_ok` on the same control was silent as a duplicate). So a stall past the hung judgement in an MSAA focus's parent walk loses the focus in NVDA; a shorter one only delays it.
