# Experiments: UiaHasServerSideProvider says no for a new conhost window while another process has an event handler

The fault's experiments `e37`, `e38` and `e39` moved to [`uia-facts/uiahasserversideprovider-says-no-for-a-new-conhost-window-while-another-process-has-an-event-handler/`](../../uia-facts/uiahasserversideprovider-says-no-for-a-new-conhost-window-while-another-process-has-an-event-handler/readme.md), and the raw probe's check to [`uia-facts/the-raw-probe-answers-0x10000-for-a-window-with-a-uia-provider/`](../../uia-facts/the-raw-probe-answers-0x10000-for-a-window-with-a-uia-provider/readme.md); their scripts, outputs and tool patches are in git at fdfb63ae. Two things stay, because lasting documents cite them:

- `kept-runs/`: three failing runs of `conhost_two_windows` from 2026-10-17 (08-04-30, 08-12-20 and 08-13-47), each with Verbatim's listener and outpost logs, Core's flight recorder and the timeline. In each, the failing probe came from a spare outpost newly given the second console's conhost, sent 1 to 3 ms after the window's foreground event, and answered no after about 3 s; in two of them the listener made no UIA call on that conhost while the probe was pending. They are the evidence of the `conhost_two_windows` inference in `books/dev/src/reference/uia-bugs.md`, which Dickson excluded from reproduction (2026-11-04).
- `design-raw-probe.md`: the proposal to replace the probe with the raw probe, written on 2026-10-18 with its amendments after `e39`, which `crates/verbatim-uia/src/probe.rs` and the fault's entry cite.
