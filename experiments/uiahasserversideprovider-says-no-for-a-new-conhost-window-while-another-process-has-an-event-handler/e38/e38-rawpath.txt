# e38 replacement path: L is a passive listener (registrations and hook,
# nothing read); V, a fresh client, sends only the raw WM_GETOBJECT probe
# and then ElementFromHandleBuildCache, and no process calls
# UiaHasServerSideProvider. All calls are waited for.
spawn L listen L e38- passive
wait L listening 20000
spawn V client 0 V
send V init
wait V done
fgright
console ${TITLE}
sleep ${DELAY}
send V target $HWND | send V rawprobe | send V efhbcd
wait V done efhbcd 20000
drain 500
closeconsole
drain 200
