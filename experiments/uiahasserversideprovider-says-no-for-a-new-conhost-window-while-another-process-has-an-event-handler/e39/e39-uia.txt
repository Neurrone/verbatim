# e39 experiment 2: under the e38 trigger (L, a passive listener: its
# registrations and hook, nothing read), V, a fresh client that has made
# its UIA client and cache request and connected to nothing, makes
# UiaHasServerSideProvider, then the lookup, DELAY ms after the console window is found. No hooks.
spawn L listen L e39- passive
wait L listening 20000
spawn V client 0 V
send V init
wait V done
fgright
console ${TITLE}
sleep ${DELAY}
send V target $HWND | send V probe | send V lookup
wait V done lookup 20000
drain 500
closeconsole
drain 200
