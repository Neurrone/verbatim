What it shows. The frame raises its MSAA focus on its client within 4 ms of its foreground event, and NVDA's defer holds both. In launches 1, 2, 4 and 5 the pump after the second deferral, 27 to 38 ms after the event, found the frame in front (no "took too long" line), and NVDA processed the frame's focus winEvent, the newest focus event of the batch, so the foreground event itself was never processed; that focus is the frame's client, a pane, spoken by its name, 0.4 to 0.6 s before the content's focus. In launch 3 the defer ran out 27 ms after the event with Program Manager still in front, NVDA processed neither of the frame's events, and "Settings" came 5 ms before "Settings window", with the content's focus: the frame's client pane entered as a focus ancestor, the object NVDA's UIA parent step makes for the frame's window with the relation "parent". The words are the same either way.
