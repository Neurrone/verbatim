# Features against design v2, part B

Claude, 2026-11-28. Read-only check at 7e981e21+1 (671aa993). Each feature is taken from `books/dev/src/reference/test-coverage.md`, worked through `docs/design/rewrite/design.md` (called "the design"), and checked against the code named. "Item N" is an item of CLAUDE.md, "Who decides".

## Assessment first

Most features in these areas fit: they are a Core entry with one or two batch asks, a shell root, or a reader-side mechanism the design already names. Three structural gaps recur and need decisions before the prototype goes past the focus slice:

1. **Requests owned by a record, not by an entry.** The design's async rule 1 says only the front entry may have a request out. Today four features send requests outside the event queue: the focused terminal's output reads, its baseline reads and its `Follow`s (`crates/verbatim-core/src/terminal/reading.rs` 1 to 18, "read at once, outside the event queue"; `crates/verbatim-core/src/wants.rs`); classic progress-bar reads (`progress.rs` 184); and the outside askers, which are the tray and taskbar list, the tree dump and the control plane (`event_queue/inbox.rs` 77 to 96, `Asker::DumpTree`, `ShellItems` and `ShellItemPosition` in `verbatim-app/src/main.rs` 1815 to 1855). The terminal model's ruling "Core alone keeps 'told, not yet read'" (Dickson, 2026-11-16) and the 4.1 ms typing echo rest on output reads that do not wait behind keys. Rule 1 has to allow requests that a feature record owns. Each such request would hold its own `Out` and `Waiter`, be resolved in `step` rather than by `await`, and belong to a scope of that record. The alternative is to make each read a queue entry, which changes the ruled order of output against keys. Rule 1 is Dickson's, so either way this is his decision.
2. **A newcomer or a settle ending the front's request.** The design's `Entry` trait has `arrive`, `foreground_changed` and `run`. Today a caret key's watch is withdrawn by a later key or by a focus that counts as pending, and a request is withdrawn when the facts settle and make it unneeded. Both go through `Logic::ends` with `Cause::Arrived` and `Cause::Settled` (`entries.rs` 624 to 646). No hook in the design does this. The fix is a fourth hook, for example `fn ends_front(&self, front: &FrontView) -> Option<CancelReason>`, plus a settle check. The hook list is from the queue-hooks proposal still pending Dickson's approval, so this is his decision.
3. **Holding scopes and reader-side waits that are not in the design.**
   - The start marker and the navigator's review position hold an outpost text anchor across focus changes. `moving_to_the_start_marker_in_another_object_is_no_start_marker_and_keeps_it` shows the marker surviving a focus change. That needs a standing scope that registers no events: a `Keep` step, which is item 4.
   - The outpost's caret watch, which re-checks over time and is brought forward by the follow's events (`verbatim-outpost-logic/src/watch.rs` 1 to 33), needs a `Request::Waiting` variant with its due time. That is Claude's choice.
   - The outpost's merge of the follow's tells, which today happens on the pipe, needs a coalesce state in the outpost like the listener's (`intake.rs` 1 to 15). That is Claude's choice, and the credit it uses is item 4.

## State in records the design deletes

- `SrState.wants`, the list of record-owned requests and their tokens, has no home in the design (addition 1).
- Each piece of state moves from the root `SrState` as follows:
  - `terminals` (Core's terminal models), `terminal_focus` and `follows` go to `Application`.
  - `edit_selection` goes to the committed `Focus` record.
  - `typed_word`, `say_all`, `start_marker` and `navigator` go to `Features`.
  - `strokes` and `arrivals` go to `Keys`.
  - `plain` goes to a Core-internal answerer (see "A ghost in front").
  - `ignored` goes to the listener's `WatchFilters`.
- The outpost's held views, its position numbering and `Carried` are deleted outright; scopes replace them.
- The outpost's text facts (`TextFacts.nodes`, anchors, last reads and kept selections) go to `Element.text`, which the design leaves "later". The caret and selection features need it from the first text build.

## Additions and misfits

1. Record-owned requests outside the front entry: the terminal's reads and follows, progress-bar reads, the tray list, the tree dump. This is a misfit without a change to rule 1 (Dickson).
2. A hook through which an arriving entry, or a settle, ends the front's request (Dickson, queue hooks).
3. A `Keep` step: a standing scope with no events, for the start marker, the navigator off the focus and say-all's anchors (item 4).
4. `ScopeId::Entry` naming any standing request, and follow items in the tell shape (item 4).
5. A `Destroyed(window)` step ordered before later requests (item 4).
6. An idle-barrier step for `AwaitIdle` (item 4).
7. Credit and coalescing of follow items in the outpost; the coalescing is Claude's choice and the credit is item 4.
8. `Request::Waiting` in the outpost for the caret and terminal watches (Claude).
9. A fourth waiter ending for the shell's pre-send hang refusal (Claude).
10. A Core-internal answerer for plain nodes (Claude).
11. Spares keyed outside `Children`, or asked by Core (Claude, or item 3).
12. `Platform` covering edit-control messages, `TVM_*` and Uniscribe, and a recorder line for edit controls (Claude).
13. The terminal models' cap of 16 across applications (Claude).
14. Speech values owned by the speech root, not by Core's `Settings` (Claude).
